## Lucide

Some of the app's system icons are Lucide drawings, copied path for path or
drawn from them: among them `Drum`, `Guitar` and `AudioWave` in
`src/components/icons.tsx`, and others in that file and in
`src/features/studio/studio-icons.tsx` that follow Lucide's 24-unit,
2-unit-stroke shapes. The Studio's instrument glyphs
(`src/features/studio/instruments/`) are the app's own, drawn on the same grid;
they copy no Lucide or Feather icon.

Source: <https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide>
