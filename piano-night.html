Piano Night — practise piano, phrase by phrase

A focused room for the keyboard. Slow a phrase down, loop the bar that will not sit, and play it back up to tempo, reading it as falling notes, as a staff or as hands on the keys. Works with a MIDI keyboard or the keys on screen.
