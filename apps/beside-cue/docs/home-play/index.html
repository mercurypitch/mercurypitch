      Reel 2 · The cast on call

      Fifteen characters, 67 recorded lines

      Every card plays its real recording with a lip-sync track generated from that audio. The mouths here are vector stand-ins over a patch of each character's own texture; the production mouths are painted into the approved art (Reel 7). Present lines stay in the Cue Moment, never on Home.

      Tap Corky on the stage in Reel 1 and he answers too. Custom Pulls stay faceless, as the product rule says; The Cue can still appear for them.

      Reel 3 · The Cue

      Pulls are things. The Cue is light.

      A cue is what brings the Pull into view: a time, a place, a feeling, or a reminder. The Cue character makes that signal visible without becoming the temptation itself. It has no face and never speaks. Each form has its own short chime, and the glow follows the chime's loudness through the same clock that drives the mouths.

      Reel 5 · Smarter cues, on the device

      A tracker that learns when to be there

      No account, no cloud, no scores. Each idea uses data the app already keeps, or one small new record, and changes nothing without the person's yes. Example data is marked as such.
