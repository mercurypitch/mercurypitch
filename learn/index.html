Find Your Pitch

Free lessons in singing and music theory, for anyone who wants to sing in tune or understand the music they hear. You need no theory to start: Lesson 1 begins with what a note is.

How a lesson works

  • It opens on a question, and you guess before you find out.
  • Sound toys let you hear each idea and play with it.
  • Every lesson asks you to sing along.
  • Short questions at the end check what stuck.

Each lesson takes about minutes.
