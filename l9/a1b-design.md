# A1b: the design the first builder settled (not built), with the lead's rulings

The first A1b builder read everything and stopped before writing code (no commits). Its design, condensed. Lead rulings are marked RULING.

1. Arc type, in band-chart-rungs.ts: `Arc { from: {t, c}, to: {t, c}, drawn?: 0..1, digit?: number }`, `BandScene.arcs?: Arc[]` (type import only). `paintRungs` paints arcs after every band and before the lit edges; arcs paint only when the scene has rungs.
2. Shape: a parabola over the span, so time stays monotonic; a polyline of about 24 steps (tests read real points). At fraction s it sits `4*rise*s*(1-s)` above the chord; `rise = |dy|/4 + dx/8` (a quarter of the climb makes it arrive level into a higher band; an eighth of the width makes even a level arc rise). Clamped below the plot's top: `rise <= (sqrt(D0) + sqrt(D1))^2 / 4`, D = each end's height below the top. Cents pinned to the window, as the trace's are. (A perpendicular-offset curve curls back over the first band on steep leaps: rejected.)
3. Partial drawing: `drawn` covers s from 0 to `drawn`, linear in x; nothing at 0.
4. Digit: plain, amber, MONO 12 px, centred, 10 px out along the chord's normal from mid-span (up-left for a leap up, up-right for a leap down), clamped inside the plot, drawn once `drawn >= 0.5`. RULING: yes, the digit waits for the apex.
5. Caps: the arc sets `lineCap = 'butt'`, `lineJoin = 'round'`, an empty dash and alpha 1 itself. With A1a's B1 fixup in place (paintRungs resets caps first), keep the arc's own settings anyway.
6. Rung mode (decision 7): `columnArcs(leaps: [c0, c1][], window)` cuts the window into equal columns in play order, numbered 1..n; each arc keeps an eighth of its column clear on each side, so two leaps that share a note (do->so, then so->top do) do not join. RULING: keep the eighth; the page's screenshot review settles it.
7. Rung names, an axis option beside `named`: `axis: { notes, names: rungNames([[50, 'do · D3'], ...]) }`; `rungNames` returns `{ gutter(ctx), paint(f, target) }`, so names cannot be given without their painter. band-chart.ts only calls the two: x0 = `Math.max(40, names?.gutter(ctx) ?? 0)`; `drawNotesGrid(f, target, named, names === undefined)`; then `names?.paint(f, target)`. `rungNames([])` gives an unlettered, unnamed grid (needs `Math.max(0, ...widths)`). Names in COLOR.ink, right-aligned at x0 - 8; rows outside the window skipped; gutter = widest measured name + 8 px each side, never under 40. RULING: yes to names carrying their painter, and yes to a third leaf module, band-chart-names.ts, with its own test (keeps Lessons 3 and 5's bytes down and test files under 400 lines).
8. band-chart-draw.ts: `drawNotesGrid` gets a `letters = true` flag (rows still drawn when off); `export` MONO. Estimated +90-100 bytes on Lessons 3 and 5 each (Lesson 3's headroom is small): measure.
9. 344 px (computed, not seen): canvas 284 x 248. With "top do · D4" (79.2 px), x0 = 96 and the plot is 176 px (232 without names); "top do · B♭3" gives x0 103, plot 169. Step 4's columns about 35 px, Step 3's slots about 44 px. "IN THE BAND" (79 px) cannot fit a four-slot live chart's slot: A1a's S2 clamp (words inside the plot) is what keeps it off the names.
10. Tests: `paintFrames(figure, count)` in the fixture (a small refactor `paintCharts` shares) and `capsOf(calls)`; leave `marksOf` alone (complexity 15). Arc cases: endpoints at `pointOf` of the band ends; interior above the chord; apex at mid-span with the digit outside it; a leap down also rises; a partial arc is a prefix ending at that fraction; the digit waits for the apex; the arc stays in the plot. Columns: Step 4's five leaps inside their fifths, covering at least three quarters of each, in order, numbered 1-5. Names: only the given rows named, no letters; the gutter fits the longest name, empty names keep 40; two frames with marks and a label draw identical name marks. Two frames with a trace: from frame 2 the arc still has butt caps.
