How do I make a karaoke version of a song?

Upload a song you own and MercuryPitch separates the vocals from the instruments. You get a karaoke-ready mix with adjustable vocal level, synced lyrics and live pitch scoring, all in your browser.

Does it work with any song?

It works with audio files from your own library. A free on-device mode runs entirely in your browser; a studio-quality server mode is available for the cleanest separation.

Can it score my singing?

Yes. Enable your microphone and MercuryPitch tracks your pitch against the original vocal melody in real time, drawing every note you sing and scoring your performance.
