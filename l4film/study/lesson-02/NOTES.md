## Rules this video follows

1. No cuts: 431 s in one continuous room. Every change of subject is a camera move on the same
   world, an object that carries (the bead), or a swap under persistent Mercs.
2. A move takes the viewer to the place; the result happens after the move settles there.
3. Pushes and pulls run 0.65 to 1.3 s, ease-in-out with a long settle; through-object moves
   accelerate in and decelerate hard out.
4. Labels never change size by riding a move they would not survive: they leave before it and land
   after it. HUD type stays in screen space.
5. A full-frame field is about half brightness with a radial falloff, never a white flash, and holds
   no more than about 0.2 to 0.3 s.
6. The next scene waits underneath the transition (the staff and notes are in place before the bead
   lands).
7. Colour code on objects: Merc's blue and Tavi's orange mark their notes and the octave's two ends;
   everything else is chrome.
8. The Mercs keep their corners and keep idling while the board between them changes.
