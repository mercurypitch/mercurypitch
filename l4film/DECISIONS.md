## Structure

1. **The cold open comes before the recall.** Its first question lands at about 0:06 and the hook
   question by about 0:34 at the voiced pace (0:33 in the scratch voices; draft 1's was 0:30,
   before the D predict and Tavi's "So home moved with the start" went in, 18), and the recall
   from Lesson 2 follows the promise, the order every page has used since 20 October
   (MASTER-PLAN § 12 item 13). Lesson 2's film asked its recall first, before that decision.
2. **The cold open is Lesson 3's card made real.** Lesson 3 hands over with "Merc climbs seven
   stairs and stops, one short of home" (Lesson 3 FACTS 62). Lesson 3's film was made in another
   session and cannot be read from this one, so the hand-over follows the written lessons.
3. **The recall is Lesson 2's question 1**, "How many semitones make an octave?", the page's
   `l02-q1`. Its answer sets up the act 1 puzzle (twelve semitones, seven steps: they cannot all
   be equal), so the recall does work instead of standing apart. The page's second recall
   question (`l02-q3`, C♯ and D♭) stays on the page.
4. **Four acts with three chapter cards**, as Lesson 2: act 1 the steps and the groups, act 2 the
   start on D and any start (card "02 Start anywhere"), act 3 the names, then ti and why do is
   home (card "03 Name the stairs"), act 4 the sing-along (card "04 Sing it").
5. **Length target 7:30 to 9:00** since draft 3 (it was 7:30 to 8:30; 22.9), inside the series'
   6 to 9 minutes. Draft 1 estimated 8:37 at Lesson 2's voiced pace, draft 2 8:32, and draft 3
   8:36 by word rate or 8:45 by Lesson 2's scratch-to-voiced ratio (BRIEF, Length target). Draft 2 took three cuts: Merc's "Let's find
   out, one step at a time" after the hook question (the title answers it), the W and H line
   (FACTS 75; the letters are not on screen either, so the film keeps one vocabulary), and "Now,
   back to the question we started with", folded into the line after it. If a voiced cut runs
   past 9:00, cut next the second slide (`slide-e` and "The shape never changes", about 5 s;
   the label keeps E's count), then the second sentence of b25 (about 3 s). The later studies'
   clause in b36 stays: "seems to be learned" rests on it (9).

## Teaching

6. **The misconception three ways**, each a sincere guess from Tavi and a demonstration: "Is that
   because we started on C?" (cold open, broken by sound), "Same white keys, just starting on D"
   (act 2, a predict, then two sounds and the dashed staircase), "In the opening, do moved up to
   D. But isn't do always C?" (act 3: she heard home move, and doubts it, which is sincere where
   draft 1's bare "So do is always C?" was not, after she had called D5 do herself; half right:
   fixed do exists, and Merc says so before the slide shows movable do).
7. **Two predicts.** Act 2's is the formal one, with a 3.0 s hold and two pills, as the page's
   step 3. The cold open's "Start on D, and listen. Will it still hang?" is asked aloud with a
   1.5 s hold and no card, as the page's cold open asks it, and Tavi says what it showed ("So home
   moved with the start", FACTS 87). Act 1's "Then some steps must be bigger than others?" is a
   guess the measuring settles, not staged as a predict.
8. **"Most listeners" is said only over a C major scale** (`stop-c-home`, `ti-do`): the listening
   studies used C major (FACTS 26). From D, the film says only the textbooks' "it leads wherever
   home is".
9. **The experiment is described by its design**, then its result, then the later studies that
   found the same order without training, only weaker, so "seems to be learned" rests on what was
   found (FACTS 81). The two are credited apart, "Krumhansl & Shepard, 1979" and "Janata et al.,
   2003", because K&S's own less musical listeners leaned on pitch height; draft 1 put the
   untrained listeners under the one experiment's credit. The tiers on screen carry no numbers.
10. **The recap interleaves**: question 3 asks Lesson 2's tone-or-semitone of the new step, ti to
    do, over a played step; question 4 asks movable do at a start the film never showed (G), so
    it tests transfer, not recall of a picture.
