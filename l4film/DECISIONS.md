## Structure

1. **The cold open comes before the recall.** The hook question lands by about 0:30, and the
   recall from Lesson 2 follows the promise, the order every page has used since 20 October
   (MASTER-PLAN § 12 item 13). Lesson 2's film asked its recall first, before that decision.
2. **The cold open is Lesson 3's card made real.** Lesson 3 hands over with "Merc climbs seven
   stairs and stops, one short of home" (Lesson 3 FACTS 62). Lesson 3's film was made in another
   session and cannot be read from this one, so the hand-over follows the written lessons.
3. **The recall is Lesson 2's question 1**, "How many semitones make an octave?", the page's
   `l02-q1`. Its answer sets up the act 1 puzzle (twelve semitones, seven steps: they cannot all
   be equal), so the recall does work instead of standing apart. The page's second recall
   question (`l02-q3`, C♯ and D♭) stays on the page.
4. **Four acts with three chapter cards**, as Lesson 2: act 1 the steps and the groups, act 2 the
   start on D and any start (card "02 Start anywhere"), act 3 the names, then ti and why do is
   home (card "03 Name the stairs"), act 4 the sing-along (card "04 Sing it").
5. **Length target 7:30 to 8:30**, inside the series' 6 to 9 minutes. Draft 1 estimates 8:37 at
   Lesson 2's voiced pace. If a voiced cut runs past 9:00, cut in this order: the W and H line

## Teaching

6. **The misconception three ways**, each a sincere guess from Tavi and a demonstration: "Is that
   because we started on C?" (cold open, broken by sound), "Same white keys, just starting on D"
   (act 2, a predict, then two sounds and the dashed staircase), "So do is always C?" (act 3,
   half right: fixed do exists, and Merc says so before the slide shows movable do).
7. **One formal predict, in act 2**, with a 3.0 s hold and two pills, as the page's step 3. The
   cold open's "Start somewhere else, and listen" and act 1's "Then some steps must be bigger?"
   are guesses the next sound or picture settles; neither is staged as a predict card.
8. **"Most listeners" is said only over a C major scale** (`stop-c-home`, `ti-do`): the listening
   studies used C major (FACTS 26). From D, the film says only the textbooks' "it leads wherever
   home is".
9. **The experiment is described by its design**, then its result, then its nuance (trained
   musicians show the order most sharply, untrained listeners show it too), so "seems to be
   learned" rests on what was found (FACTS 81). The tiers on screen carry no numbers.
10. **The recap interleaves**: question 3 asks Lesson 2's tone-or-semitone of the new step, ti to
    do, over a played step; question 4 asks movable do at a start the film never showed (G), so
    it tests transfer, not recall of a picture.

## Sound and voice

11. **Every sung note is the synthesised "ooh"** of the app's demo voice (FACTS 67): the runs need
    every note of D major and C major, and the Echo samples hold only A3 and A4.
12. **The cold open's two runs share an octave** (C4 and D4 starts, as the page's `stop-c` and
    `stop-d`), so the only difference the ear gets is where the scale starts.
13. **The sing-along is split by rule 16**: Merc sings the first half from D3, Tavi the second
    half an octave up (the climb to ti, the hold, home), and the viewer the whole, 18.2 s with
    one count-in note. The bed is an open fifth on D, which gives the viewer's starting note.
14. **The care lines are voiced; the doctor lines are not.** "Sore, hoarse or unwell today? Just
    listen along.", "at about speaking volume" and "Stop immediately if anything feels
    uncomfortable." come before the turn, in full (the short form was for Lessons 1 and 2 only);
    the doctor lines go in the YouTube description and stay on the page.
15. **Solfège is spelled for the voice** ("doh, ray, mee, fah, soh, lah, tee"), written as the
    picture writes it in captions (FACTS 66).
16. **Scratch voices first, final voices on the owner's word.** Kokoro voices every draft; the
    ElevenLabs re-voice spends the owner's credits, so it waits for an explicit yes, and is never
    re-run to retry a take.
