# Storyboard v3.1 (approved for build): Find Your Pitch, Lesson 4, "The major scale and home"

1920x1080, 60 fps, 8:03.7 (483.73 s) on the scratch clock (`cues.json`, Kokoro voices), about
8:41 to 8:50 once voiced (BRIEF, Length target). Every time below is a cue; scenes key off cue names
(`L.at(scene, cue)`), never off typed seconds, so the voiced pass re-times everything. The script is
draft 3 (`SCRIPT-DRAFT.md`, `SCRIPT.md`) with two pauses the storyboard asked for: `hook`, 1 s after
the cold open's question, and `card`, 4 s on the practise card. Exact pixels for every carried
object, every framing and the geometry formulas are in [HANDOFFS.md](HANDOFFS.md); this board names
framings and positions, HANDOFFS fixes them.

## The world

Lessons 1 and 2's continuous dark listening room: Obsidian `#0d1117`, three soft radial glows (blue
top-left, violet top-right, aqua low-left), the stats site's faint five-line staves at 3.5 % as the
far layer, seeded dust motes as the near layer, with a few soft, faint bokeh discs of near dust that
move only with the camera, at 1.6 times the world, and brighten with the staves while they slide
against it (DECISIONS 89, 93). When the Mercs are on screen a floor horizon sits at
y 840 with a soft wet reflection of each (their own passes, flipped and blurred, 16 %). Camera moves
are `L.rig` on far, mid and near layers (0.25, 1, 1.6): a push, a pull or a truck shows parallax; a
pan moves every layer by the same amount. The Mercs are placed in screen space in every scene, so no
camera move shifts or scales them.

The diagram keeps clear of the Mercs in every framing (HANDOFFS § World): the faint semitone rows
are masked in screen space round each Merc on screen (fully clear within 20 px of the body, back to
full over the next 60 px), and in a framing that would carry the keyboard strip under a Merc's
feet, the strip fades as the camera moves in and returns as it pulls out. Stairs, keys and labels
are never masked: every framing that holds a Merc keeps them 20 px clear by its numbers.

The framings (HANDOFFS § Framings):

- **F-cold**, the cold open's: the compact staircase centred, scale 1.2, both Mercs either side,
  drifting to 1.22 through the cold open; **F-house**, 2.2x with the top house at the frame's
  centre, where S1's pass-through ends.
- **F0**, the wide shot, scale 1: the whole diagram (semitone rows, staircase, keyboard strip), which
  holds every start from C to G without rescaling, so a slide along the keyboard is an object move,
  never a camera move.
- **Close-ups**, 1.8x to 2x, each with no Merc in its corner unless the numbers allow one: **CU-A**
  and **CU-B** on the first steps (S5c, S5d), **CU-D** on D's stairs 3 to 7, where the white keys
  leave the pattern (S7g), and **CU-C** on la, ti and do (S10a).
- **Medium shots**, 1.25x to 1.8x, each for one beat: **F-low** and **F-high** on the scale's two
  halves (S6b), **F-white** on Tavi's white-keys staircase (S7d), **F-keys-D** on D major's two
  black keys (S7h), **F-near** on the staircase as it gets its names (S9b, S9c), **F-exp** and **F-chart** on
  the experiment's chart, the second filling the frame (S11), **F-recap-1** and **F-recap-3** on the staircase under recap cards 1
  and 3 (S14).
- **F-names**, 2.4x on the stairs' feet and their names, trucking from stair 1 to stair 8 (S9d).
- **The guide** (S12, S13): 13 semitone lanes, D3 to D4, time running left to right.

Every span longer than about 0.5 s has something that moves or changes: a Merc on screen, an object
or label moving, appearing or leaving, a stair or key lighting, a camera or object move while content
in frame travels at least about 8 px a second, or a counted pulse (§ Type). A drift, a breathing glow
and the near dust do not count. In a close-up with no Merc one movement leads under the pulses (S5c to
S5e: a push, a pan of 480 px, a pull).

Captions: nothing essential below y 896 in any shot (the bottom 17 % is the caption band), and in S13
nothing of the care block below y 864 (the bottom fifth, BRIEF). Delivery as Lessons 1 and 2: a clean
master plus SRT and VTT from `cues.json`.

## Persistent actors

1. **The staircase.** One object the whole way: eight stairs, do to do, each a block from its tread
   down to a foot 46 px below do's row, standing on faint semitone rows (Lesson 2's equal tiles,
   stood upright in S4). It is compact and bare in the cold open (F-cold: names only, no keyboard),
   gains Lesson 2's keyboard as a strip under it (S5), slides along that strip to D, G and E without
   changing shape and back to C (S7, S8), switches its labels from letters to numbers to do-re-mi
   (S9), regroups into tiers for the experiment and settles back (S11), tips into the guide's lanes
   at full brightness (S12), tips back out of them across the breath (S13d), carries the recap (S14)
   and ends as Scale Runner's ladder (S14f), whose top rung, do, carries across the cut into S15's
   bead. Every change happens on screen, never across a cut and
   never under a chapter card.
2. **The beads.** The blue bead is Merc's sung note and the orange bead Tavi's (Lessons 1 and 2's
   code): they climb the treads with each sung run (S1, S10) and ride the guide's path (S12). The
   count-in's blue bead (S13) gives the viewer Merc's note. A **chrome bead** (`#c3ccd6`) climbs the
   treads with every soft-tone example (S5, S6, S7, S8, S14). A bead rests on its tread, and a hop
   rises before it travels, so it clears the riser labels. A bead that lands on a do stair under a
   house rises into the house and becomes its light (S1e, S10c), as the title's bead does at 37.6;
   in S14d the bead hops from ti straight into the unlit house, which lights as it enters. On the
   end card the blue and orange beads bob gently beside the lockup.
3. **The house glyph.** Do, home, is a house (an SVG icon, never a colour): unlit, a chrome outline;
   lit, an ink fill with a soft chrome glow. In the cold open it sits only over the top stair, the
   one the climb is heading for; from S9b ("its home") it sits over both do stairs and rides every
   slide, except while Merc's run climbs from the bottom do in S10 (281.5 to 296.3), when the bottom
   house fades out so the bead on stair 1 never overlaps it.

## Signature moments

1. **One short of home** (S1a to S1f). Merc's blue bead climbs seven stairs with his voice and stops;
   the eighth stair waits above it, an outline under an unlit house. The whole staircase rises two
   rows, Tavi's orange bead climbs it and hangs again, and her last step lands: the top stair fills,
   and the bead rises into the house and becomes its light.
2. **The pattern slides, the keys change** (S8b to S8d). The staircase, its two gold short steps in
   place, glides along the keyboard from D to G, then to E, never a stair out of line, while the
   black keys under it light one, then four; three ghost outlines of where it stood slide back onto
   it, the same shape every time. The camera does not move.
3. **The names ride the stairs** (S9f). do, re, mi stay on their stairs as the staircase slides to
   D; under them the keys D, E and F♯ are named one by one, and the house moves with do.

## Type

As Lessons 1 and 2. Kickers JetBrains Mono 500, 30 px, uppercase, tracking .18em, ink-2, at (120,
110). Big ideas Instrument Serif italic, 96 to 170 px. Numbers Outfit 600 tabular. Diagram labels
Outfit 600, 36 to 64 px. ♯ and ♭ are Noto Music glyphs drawn as paths. On-screen text is a label, a
number or the key term, never a whole narrated sentence, except the care block's stop line and the
doctor lines (S13), which are carried whole.

Frame-fixed type (kickers, the list, legends, tallies, sums, cards, the hook's two lines) never
scales and never rides a camera move; a label that belongs to the diagram (a stair's name, a riser's
label, a key's name, "tonic = home", "(sol)", the brackets) rides the world and scales with it. A
frame-fixed label that a framing would put over the diagram leaves before the move and its
replacement lands after it (RULES 4).

Chapter cards (the act 2, 3 and 4 breaths) are Lesson 1's: the act numeral in aqua, Instrument Serif
italic 300 px, beside the act title in chrome serif italic 170 px, centred on baseline y 480, in from
act + 0.22 (0.85 s, expo.out), breathing from scale 1 to 1.03 over its hold, leaving up fast (0.38 s,
power3.in) 0.1 s before the next line, over the staircase dimmed to 25 % (its labels hidden while it
is dim). The staircase never changes shape under a card. Act 1 has a kicker only. Every kicker lands
with its act's first line after the card and leaves at the end of its second line, unless a row
names another cue.

Timing of text and marks: unless a row says otherwise, a label or mark fades in over 0.2 s from its
cue and out over 0.2 s from its exit cue; "up fast" is 0.3 s, power3.in. Nothing lands where something
else is still leaving: a replacement starts once the one before has gone. A label switch (letters to
numbers, T to 2) slides the old label up 12 px as it fades and the new one in from 12 px below,
0.25 s, staggered 0.05 s stair by stair. A ring timer leaves as its pause ends.

Counted pulses (Lesson 2's, measured by its critic 08): a key or a stair block scales 1.05; a riser
bar grows 15 % in height; a label of 64 px or more scales 1.2, each about its own centre, up over
0.15 s and back over 0.6 s (power2.out, then sine.inOut), credited 0.7 s; a bead scales 1.2 or sends
an echo ring (a 2 px chrome ring growing from its edge to three times its radius as it fades over
0.5 s), credited 0.5 s; a stair block lit to 40 % chrome (or 45 % blue or orange), or a black key
lit to 40 % chrome, is a large area, credited 0.7 s once the lab clip shows the 40 % mix registering
as a change. Glows, halos and a house lighting are accents and never stand in for motion. Rows say
"scales", "grows", "echo ring" or "lights" for a counted event, and "glows" for an accent.

Dim strokes and fills, never labels: every settled label stays at 4.5:1 or better against what is
behind it (on the ground ink measures 16.0:1 and ink-2 8.9:1; ink on an unlit stair `#1b232d`
13.4:1, on a stair lit 40 % chrome 4.9:1, on a black key lit 40 % chrome 5.2:1, on a stair lit 45 %
blue 5.7:1 and 45 % orange 6.1:1; each is measured again in the lab, and a mix that measures under
4.5:1 there is lowered until it passes). A label whose group dims is hidden while the group is dim
(the staircase under the chapter cards, in S4 and in S11's tiers).
