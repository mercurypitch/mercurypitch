fix(native): Try each example stem again when its download stalls

Every native build fetches the Karaoke room's six example stems from the
public R2 bucket (sync-native-assets.mjs calls ensureKaraokeExamples), and
each was fetched once, with no time limit, its body read outside any catch.
On 28 October the bucket hung some transfers for minutes and then dropped
them: on #1242 the PR gate's Mercury Pitch build waited 5 min 41 s on the
first stem and failed with `TypeError: terminated`, and the iOS and Android
builds 11 and 20 min, while iOS Release, in the same run, fetched all six in
about a minute.

Each file now gets three tries, 5 and then 10 s apart. A try is abandoned
once 30 s pass with nothing arriving, before the answer or between any two
parts of its body, so a slow transfer that is still moving is never cut off.
A 5xx or a 429 is tried again; any other answer that is not ok stops at
once, as before. Each failed try is logged, and the build's error names the
tries. The pin check and the `.part` file are as they were.

Tests, six new in karaoke-examples.test.ts: a fetch that fails and then
succeeds; a body that drops part-way; a 503 and then the file, and a 404
tried once; three failures, named, with nothing written; and, against a
server on the machine, a transfer that stalls after two bytes is abandoned
and tried again, while one that sends a byte every 100 ms under a 300 ms
limit is not cut off. With one try, with no reset per part, with no signal,
with a 404 tried again, or with a body error thrown past the retry, at least
one of them fails.
