test(e2e): Serve the demo song to the Jam stage layout spec

Every test in jam-stage-layout.spec.ts opens Goodbye to Spring, and the
room takes a song only once its words have arrived: exampleSong awaits
demoLyricsText, which fetches lyrics.lrc from the public R2 bucket, and
the stems stream from the same bucket. The spec routed neither. On
#1243's CI, while the bucket stalled transfers, one of its tests failed
all three tries and five passed only on a retry. With no route to the
bucket at all, as in a sandbox, the song has no words and every test
fails.

The spec now serves the song itself before each test, as
jam-control-bar.spec.ts does. lyrics.lrc is the gold copy in
fixtures/lrc, the same words the bucket serves. Each stem is a 220 Hz
tone as long as the song, 246 s, served in byte ranges: the room parks
on line 6, 56 s in, and an element that cannot seek lands every jump on
zero.

With every r2.dev request aborted, the 14 tests pass and none tries the
bucket. Without the new routes, all 14 fail waiting for line 6, and 42
requests try it.
