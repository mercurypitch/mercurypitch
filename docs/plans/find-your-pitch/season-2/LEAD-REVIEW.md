   into tune. True for a unison or an octave; above a drone they stop only at the pure intervals.
   Arithmetic on harmonic tones over A3 (220 Hz): the equal-tempered fifth (E4 329.63) beats 0.745
   times a second (659.26 against 660 Hz), the equal-tempered major third (C#4 277.18) 8.73 times
   a second (1,108.73 against 1,100 Hz); over C3, 5.19. So the lesson's central claim needed its
   condition, the signature its pure/tempered difference, and the facts a source on beating
   partials (to find). Risk 5 (Drone Intonation scores a pure third about 79) stands.

   steps but two three-semitone risers, which Lesson 4's staircase would show.
