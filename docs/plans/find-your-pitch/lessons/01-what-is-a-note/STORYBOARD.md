# Storyboard v3: Find Your Pitch, Lesson 1, "What is a note?"

1920x1080, 60 fps, 4:53 (293.2 s; re-timed after the b16a re-voice), timed to the narration clock in `cues.json`. Every time below
is a cue; scenes key off cue names (`L.at(scene, cue)`), never off typed seconds. v2 answers
`review/critic-01/report.md` item by item (noted as [c1.n]).

## The world

One continuous dark listening room: obsidian `#0d1117`, three soft radial glows (blue top-left,
violet top-right, aqua low-left), the stats site's faint five-line staves at 3.5% as the far
layer, seeded dust motes as the near layer. When Mercs are on screen a floor horizon sits at
y = 840 with a soft wet reflection of each Merc (their own passes, flipped and blurred, 16%).
Camera moves are `L.camera` on far / mid / near layers (0.25 / 1 / 1.6), so a push always shows
parallax.

Captions [c1.11]: nothing essential below y = 896 in any shot (the bottom 17% is the caption band).
Delivery: a clean master plus an SRT/VTT built from `cues.json`, and a captioned master with the
same captions burned into the caption band by ffmpeg (no re-render). On mute the captioned master
carries the whole narration.

## Persistent actors

1. **The two beads.** Blue bead = blue Merc's note (A3, 220 Hz); orange bead = orange Merc's note
   (A4, 440 Hz). They appear in the cold open and keep that meaning everywhere: they lead the two
   traces (S8 to S10), stack on the spiral (S13 to S15), land on the A3 and A4 keys (S16), lead the
   two singing lines (S17, S18), answer the recap (S19) and orbit the end card. **A chrome bead**
   (white body, chrome rim, no colour) is any other note: the sweep (S5), the ladder (S12), the
   climbing note on the spiral (S13) and the letters (S14) [c1.7].
2. **The line.** The title staff's middle line becomes the plucked string (S3); the string's
   midpoint writes the trace (S4); the trace is the wave of S5 to S11.
3. **The rail.** S12's right-hand ruler ("as you hear it") winds into the spiral (S13); the spiral
   tips over and unrolls into the keyboard, low notes left (S16) [c1.10]; the keyboard turns 90
   degrees to become the piano-roll keys of S17 and S18, A keys feeding A lanes [c1.10].

## Signature moments

1. **The line becomes a spiral** (S13): the straight ruler winds into a turning spiral; the A beads
   end up stacked one above the other on the same side.
2. **Two steps for one** (S10): the Mercs bounce in place, orange twice for every blue bounce, and
   a gold ring flashes under both on every shared landing.
3. **The spiral lies down and becomes a piano** (S16): it tips so its top points right, flattens,
   and its letter beads drop into keys; the blue and orange beads land on A3 and A4.

## Codes

- Colour: blue `#58a6ff` = blue Merc's note; orange `#ff7448` = orange Merc's note; gold `#ffd84d`
  = things that line up (never "the right answer" for a perception) [c1.3]; violet `#bc8cff` = the
  contrast note (277 Hz); chrome `#c3ccd6` = any other note; ink `#e6edf3` labels. Every coloured
  thing also has a label.
- Pitch geometry used everywhere: one octave = 200 px of height on a rail; on the spiral, 30 deg per
  semitone, 360 deg per octave, A at 0 deg (front), so A 0, B 60, C 90, D 150, E 210, F 240, G 300
  deg; the five unnamed notes at 30, 120, 180, 270, 330 deg [c1.2]. Octave numbers change at C
  [c1.1]: the "4" turn starts at C4, 90 deg past A3.
- Waves: every trace is a cosine (a peak at its left edge) travelling right at the same speed, so
  peaks of a 2:1 pair coincide at every slow peak [c1.5]:
  `y = mid - amp * cos(2 pi * cyc * (x - x0 - 60 * T) / W)`, T = film time, W = x1 - x0.
  A faint time axis (hairline, "time" in mono) sits under every trace that is read as a graph.

## Type

Kickers JetBrains Mono 500, 30 px, uppercase, tracking .18em, ink-2. Big ideas Instrument Serif
italic, 160 to 300 px. Numbers Outfit 600 tabular, 96 to 180 px, units JetBrains Mono. Diagram
labels Outfit 600, 48 to 64 px. On-screen text never repeats a whole narrated sentence.
