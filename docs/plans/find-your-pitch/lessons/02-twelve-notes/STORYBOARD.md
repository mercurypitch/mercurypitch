# Storyboard v2: Find Your Pitch, Lesson 2, "Twelve notes"

1920x1080, 60 fps, about 6:54 (414.2 s), timed to the estimated cue sheet (SCRIPT.md: lines at 2.6
words a second, every other event at its `dur`). Every time below is a cue; scenes key off cue
names (`L.at(scene, cue)`), never off typed seconds, so the first voice pass re-times everything.
v1 had one storyboard critic round (`output/motion/lesson-02/review/critic-01/`, ONE MORE PASS)
and one script critic round (`review/script-01/`); v2 answers both (DECISIONS, "Round 1
response"). The verification round comes next (PRODUCTION-PIPELINE step 7).

## The world

Lesson 1's continuous dark listening room: Obsidian `#0d1117`, three soft radial glows (blue
top-left, violet top-right, aqua low-left), the stats site's faint five-line staves at 3.5 % as the
far layer, seeded dust motes as the near layer. When the Mercs are on screen a floor horizon sits at
y 840 with a soft wet reflection of each Merc (their own passes, flipped and blurred, 16 %). Camera
moves are `L.camera` on far, mid and near layers (0.25, 1, 1.6), so a push always shows parallax.

Captions: nothing essential below y 896 in any shot (the bottom 17 % is the caption band). Delivery
as Lesson 1: a clean master plus SRT and VTT from `cues.json`.

## Persistent actors

1. **The two beads.** Blue bead = blue Merc's note (A3, 220 Hz); orange bead = orange Merc's note
   (A4, 440 Hz). They land on the spiral's front stack in the cold open and keep that meaning: they
   open and close the twelve-step climb (S5), sit on the A3 and A4 keys (S7, S8), edge the first
   and last tiles (S9 to S13), mark 0 ¢ and 1200 ¢ on the ruler (S13) and orbit the end card
   (S17). **Chrome beads** (`#c3ccd6`) are every other note; a dim chrome bead with a "?" is one of
   the five unnamed notes until it lights in S5.
2. **The spiral, then the keyboard, the tiles, the ruler and the lanes.** One object the whole way:
   Lesson 1's spiral (S1 to S6) unrolls into the keyboard (S7, S8), the keyboard straightens into
   equal tiles (S9 to S12), the tile row stands up into a vertical ruler of cents (S13), and the
   ruler's lanes extend into the pitch canvas (S14). Every change happens on screen, never across
   a cut, and each handoff between builders is a frozen frame in [HANDOFFS.md](HANDOFFS.md).
3. **Step bars.** A vertical bar over each heard step, 80 px per semitone on screen and 24 px wide,
   drawn in the top layer so a camera push never scales them (pitch is height, playbook rule 20),
   from the predict onwards: one notch for a semitone, two for a tone, each labelled "1" or "2".
   They become the "1" and "2" brackets under the tiles.

## Signature moments

1. **The ring of twelve** (S5b, S6b). As the climb plays, the five dim beads on the lit turn light
   up one by one; the count reaches twelve as A comes back one turn up. Then the camera rises and
   looks straight down the spiral: the turn is a ring of twelve beads, evenly spaced like the hours
   on a clock, and twelve equal wedges light in gold.
2. **The keyboard drawn fair** (S9d, S10c). Every key slides into one row at the same width, and B
   and C turn out to touch, as close as C and its black key. Then the five dark tiles lift out,
   leaving the seven letters with five holes a note wide and two touching pairs, and drop back in:
   that is where the other five come from.
3. **One key, two names** (S11c). An arrow climbs one semitone from C and writes C♯ on the black
   tile; another steps one semitone down from D and writes D♭ on the same tile; both names stay,
   side by side, under one gold ring.

## Codes

- Colour (explanatory diagrams, MASTER-PLAN § 5): blue `#58a6ff` = blue Merc's note; orange
  `#ff7448` = orange Merc's note; gold `#ffd84d` = the fit only (the octave's "× 2", the equal
  wedges, the matching step bars, the one-key ring, "E♯ = F", "1200 ¢ = 1 octave"), never "the
  right answer" (an answer pill gets a check icon and ink); chrome `#c3ccd6` = any other note; ink
  `#e6edf3` labels. Every coloured thing also has a label.
- Orange's wrong guesses are drawn as guesses: dashed, in ink-2 `#a8b3bf`, with a "?" (the seven
  rings in S1e, the B-to-C arc in S8b, "black = sharps?" in S12a). Lesson 2 does not use the
  series' violet for them, because violet on the pitch canvas is the app's singer colour and
  orange's own correct slide in S14 (DECISIONS 38).
- Pitch-canvas scenes (S14, and the S16 preview) use the app's canvas set: target amber `#f59e0b`
  (blue Merc's model note is the amber target band, never blue), singer violet `#a78bfa`
  (orange's sung line), as MASTER-PLAN § 5 requires.
- Spiral geometry, Lesson 1's: three turns, A2 at the bottom to A5 at the top, radius 300, 200 px
  per turn (16.67 px per semitone), 30 degrees per semitone, A at 0 degrees (the front), so A 0, A♯
  30, B 60, C 90, C♯ 120, D 150, D♯ 180, E 210, F 240, F♯ 270, G 300, G♯ 330. Octave numbers change
  at C (Lesson 1's C seams). The seven letter gaps are A-B, B-C, C-D, D-E, E-F, F-G and G-A; the
  five dim beads sit in the five that are 60 degrees wide.
- Keyboard: Lesson 1's A2 to A5 keyboard (HANDOFFS § "Carried from Lesson 1"); one-octave close-up
  = a uniform 2x push about (960, 820), so A3 to A4 fills x 407 to 1513.
- Tiles: one tile per semitone, 13 tiles from A3 to A4, centres x = 240 + 120 i, the black keys'
  tiles dark. Brackets under the row: "1" per neighbouring pair, "2" for a tone.
- Ruler: vertical, low at the bottom: A3 at y 820 to A4 at y 160 (55 px per semitone), cents on
  the right, names on the left in the pitch line's sharp spelling, under the house label "AS ON
  MERCURYPITCH'S PITCH LINE".

a label, a number or the key term, never a whole narrated sentence.
