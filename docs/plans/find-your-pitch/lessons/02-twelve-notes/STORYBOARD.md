# Storyboard v11 (approved for build): Find Your Pitch, Lesson 2, "Twelve notes"

1920x1080, 60 fps, about 6:50 (410.2 s), timed to the estimated cue sheet (SCRIPT.md: lines at 2.6
words a second, every other event at its `dur`). Every time below is a cue; scenes key off cue names
(`L.at(scene, cue)`), never off typed seconds, so the first voice pass re-times everything. v1 to v9
each had a storyboard critic round (`output/motion/lesson-02/review/critic-01/` to `critic-09/`, all
ONE MORE PASS), and v1 and v2 a script critic round (`review/script-01/`, `script-02/`, the second
SHIP). Critic 10 said SHIP on v10 (`review/critic-10/`), so this board is approved for build: v11
makes the five text changes it listed (DECISIONS, "Round 10 response"), with the script's words and
timing unchanged since round 2. No storyboard round follows; pixel-level checks belong to the
component rounds (§ Build checks; PRODUCTION-PIPELINE steps 8 and 9), and critic 10's four questions
for a human stay open for the owner (§ Approved for build).

## The world

Lesson 1's continuous dark listening room: Obsidian `#0d1117`, three soft radial glows (blue
top-left, violet top-right, aqua low-left), the stats site's faint five-line staves at 3.5 % as the
far layer, seeded dust motes as the near layer. When the Mercs are on screen a floor horizon sits at
y 840 with a soft wet reflection of each Merc (their own passes, flipped and blurred, 16 %). Camera
moves are `L.camera` on far, mid and near layers (0.25, 1, 1.6), so a push, a pull or a dolly always
shows parallax; a tilt moves every layer by the same amount, so it reads as a turn of the camera,
never a pedestal, and so does a pan, sideways: every pan below (S9a to S9d, S10e, S12b and S12c)
moves every layer by the same amount, even the two made by moving a push's centre. A truck moves the
camera itself, so the layers slide at their own rates and the parallax shows it (S11b to S11d, the
board's one truck). An arc travels the camera round its subject, so the far layer slides with the
camera and the near layer against it; a turntable turn spins the object while the camera and every
layer hold. Every span longer than about 0.5 s has something that moves or changes: a Merc on
screen, an object or label moving, appearing or leaving, a value changing, a camera or object move
while content in frame travels at least about 8 px a second, or a counted pulse on a word (§ Type).
Moves count by their speed, not their size: a 150 px pan over 16 s (sine.inOut) counts in its middle
two thirds, a 4 % push over 7 s only mid-span, and a 2 % drift never, so a drift, a breathing glow
and the near dust do not count, because measure.py reads them as holds (DECISIONS 76). In a close-up
with no Merc one movement leads under the pulses (motion principle 3): a pan, a truck or a slide of
at least 120 px, never a drift or a hold. The Mercs ride the near layer in the cold open, so its
pushes slide them outward; everywhere else they are placed in screen space, and no camera move
shifts or scales them. Every change of shot size below names its scale and its curve. On the spiral
1x is a WS and about 2x a CU; on the keyboard and the tiles the octave frame (S8) is an MS and a
push of 2x or more on it a CU.

Captions: nothing essential below y 896 in any shot (the bottom 17 % is the caption band). Delivery
as Lesson 1: a clean master plus SRT and VTT from `cues.json`.

## Persistent actors

1. **The two beads.** Blue bead = blue Merc's note (A3, 220 Hz); orange bead = orange Merc's note
   (A4, 440 Hz). They land on the spiral's front stack in the cold open and keep that meaning: they
   open and close the twelve-step climb (S5), sit on the A3 and A4 keys (S7, S8), edge the first
   and last tiles (S9 to S13), mark 0 ¢ and 1200 ¢ on the ruler (S13) and orbit the end card
   (S17). **Chrome beads** (`#c3ccd6`) are every other note; a dim chrome bead with a "?" is one of
   the five unnamed notes until it lights in S5.
2. **The spiral, then the keyboard, the tiles, the ruler and the lanes.** One object the whole way:
   Lesson 1's spiral (S1 to S6) unrolls into the keyboard (S7, S8), the keyboard straightens into
   equal tiles (S9 to S12), the tile row stands up into a vertical ruler of cents (S13), and the
   ruler's lanes extend into the pitch canvas (S14). Every change happens on screen, never across
   a cut, and each handoff between builders is a frozen frame in [HANDOFFS.md](HANDOFFS.md).
3. **Step bars.** A vertical bar over each heard step, 80 px per semitone on screen and 24 px wide,
   drawn in the top layer so a camera push never scales them (pitch is height, playbook rule 20),
   from the predict onwards: one notch for a semitone, two for a tone, each labelled "1" or "2".
   They become the "1" and "2" brackets under the tiles.

## Signature moments

1. **The ring of twelve** (S5b, S6b). As the climb plays, the five dim beads on the lit turn light
   up one by one; the count reaches twelve as A comes back one turn up. Then the camera rises and
   looks straight down the spiral: the turn is a ring of twelve beads, evenly spaced like the hours
   on a clock, and twelve equal wedges light in gold.
2. **The keyboard drawn fair** (S9d, S10c to S10e). Every key slides into one row at the same width,
   and B and C turn out to touch, as close as C and its black key. Then the five dark tiles lift
   out, leaving the seven letters with five holes a note wide and two touching pairs, and drop back
   in: that is where the other five come from.
3. **One key, two names** (S11c). An arrow climbs one semitone from C and writes C♯ on the black
   tile; another steps one semitone down from D and writes D♭ on the same tile; both names stay,
   side by side, under one gold ring.

## Type

As Lesson 1. Kickers JetBrains Mono 500, 30 px, uppercase, tracking .18em, ink-2, at (120, 110). Big
ideas Instrument Serif italic, 120 to 300 px; a key term gets one full-frame moment, as Lesson 1's
"octave" did. Numbers Outfit 600 tabular, 64 to 160 px, units in JetBrains Mono. Diagram labels
Outfit 600, 48 to 64 px; key and tile labels JetBrains Mono, dark ink `#2a323c` on white keys
(11:1). ♯ and ♭ are Noto Music glyphs drawn as paths. Chapter cards (the act 2, 3 and 4 breaths) are
Lesson 1's (its s08 act 2 card): the act numeral in aqua `#2dd4bf`, Instrument Serif italic 300 px,
beside the act title in chrome serif italic 170 px, in from act + 0.22 (0.85 s, expo.out), breathing
from scale 1 to 1.03 over its hold (about 3 % a second, fast enough to read as motion, unlike a 2 %
drift over many seconds), and leaving up fast (0.38 s, power3.in) 0.1 s before the next line. A card
never sits over the object it introduces: the act 2 card lands over a spiral dimmed to 25 %, and the
act 3 and 4 cards sit above the tile row (baseline y 360). Every kicker leaves on a named cue
(Lesson 1's rule: an act's kicker lands with the act's first line after the card, or once the card
has gone if it would land on it, and leaves at the end of the act's second line, or sooner when a
label needs its lane); each row names it. On-screen text is a label, a number or the key term, never
a whole narrated sentence.

Timing of text and marks, for every row: unless a row says otherwise, a label or mark fades in over
0.2 s from its cue and out over 0.2 s from its exit cue, and "up fast" is 0.3 s, power3.in (a
chapter card keeps Lesson 1's 0.38 s). Nothing lands where something else is still leaving: a
replacement starts only once the element before it has gone, and the rows give both times. A ring
timer leaves as its pause ends. A counted pulse moves an object's edges or changes a large area,
because measure.py reads nothing else (critic 08 measured mock pulses): a tile or key scales 1.05, a
bar grows 15 % in height, and a label of 64 px or more scales 1.2, each about its own centre, rising
over 0.15 s and settling over 0.6 s, credited 0.7 s; a bead scales 1.2 or sends an echo ring (a 2 px
chrome ring growing from its edge to three times its radius as it fades), credited 0.5 s; a dark
tile lit to 60 % chrome, or a key face brightened by 20 luma at 2x or more, is a large area,
credited 0.7 s; a long pulse settles over 0.8 s, credited 0.85 s. Glows, halos, edge flashes and a
white key or tile lit at 1x are accents: they never count. Rows say "scales", "grows", "echo ring"
or "lights" (a large area) for a counted event, and "glows" or "flashes" for an accent. Kickers,
"PITCH-LINE NAMES" and "CENTS ABOVE A3" are tracked .18em; every other mono label is set solid.
Every mark has an exit cue in its row or the next; nothing essential sits below y 896, on a tile's
edge, or within 20 px of a Merc or an unrelated object; an object dimmed to 25 % or less behind big
type is its background, not its neighbour (the act 2 card, "the other five?"). Dim strokes and
fills, never labels: every settled label stays at 4.5:1 or better against what is behind it (on the
ground ink measures 16:1, ink-2 8.9:1 and ink at 55 % 5.4:1, the quietest level this board uses),
and a label whose group dims is either hidden while the group is dim (S11b's row, S14g's guide) or
set at a level that passes (S14f's octave-up names, S15g's ghosted pads). Lesson 1's film failed
this rule in its final round, its dimmed labels measuring 2.2 to 3.4:1 (PRODUCTION-PIPELINE § 4,
trap 12).
