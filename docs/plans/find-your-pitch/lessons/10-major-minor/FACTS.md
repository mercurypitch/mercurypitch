## Unsupported, not used

- "A bright major chord" and "a dark minor one", the season's old hook's words: no study read
  measured "bright" or "dark" for these chords. One abstract describes the major mode as
  "typically used to convey excited, happy, bright or martial emotions" in Western music (Bowling
  and colleagues, 2012, PLOS ONE 7(3), e31942, PMID 22431970, abstract read), a description of
  use, not of how the chords sound to everyone. Left out of the title (1); the old wording stays
  only as the admin console's alternative.

  music are the "sad" ones, and no source read gives a colour pairing free of that link. The page
  draws the change as light and shape (40).

  and MASTER-PLAN § 12 item 12 (`MASTER-PLAN.md:494`): the Wikipedia articles read say movable do is
  "a fundamental element of the Kodály method" (Solfège) and describe Kodály's sequence starting

  children" (29).
