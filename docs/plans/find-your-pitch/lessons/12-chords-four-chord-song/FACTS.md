## Unsupported, not used

  pop songs from the last few decades" and shows a video in which "dozens of them are combined
  together", but counts none; the Hooktheory blog's analysis of "over 1300" songs reports chord
  frequencies (that F and G "are used just as often" as C in C, and which chord tends to follow
  which) but no count of songs on I–V–vi–IV. So the page makes no count, and the owner dropped the
  `hundreds` hook on 22 October 2026 (1).
- "The most common chord progression", Ableton's heading for 1-5-6-4: no count behind it in the
  page read. Not used; the page quotes only Open Music Theory's "probably the most common chords",
  attributed (28).
