## Unsupported, not used

- How readers see or group a line: the plan's "the eye takes in a group", and the encyclopedia's
  uncited "chunking"; not claimed (24). How far ahead fluent readers look: not verified (R18-4).
- That patterns teach sight-singing better than names: Henry's study had no group taught another way
  (22; RESEARCH, where a source contradicts the card, item 2).
- The plan's three Go deeper items rest on sources read and are cut for length (3): the exam's
  one-bar tap (19, said in Step 3 instead), Mishra's finding that sight-reading "generally improved
  pre- to posttest for both control and treatment groups" (R18-2, Moderate, abstract only), and
  Henry's familiar tunes, which taught as well as new ones (R18-1). Any may return if a later edit
  frees the words.

  helped): the card's line on them is left out with the item above; its signed effect sizes are not
  quoted anywhere (RESEARCH, where a source contradicts the card, item 3).
