# Dense source and glass material review

Source preservation and visual acceptance are separate gates. A fresh-process
reopen audit establishes that the packed project contains the expected topology
and full maps. It does not establish correct glass optics or game performance.
All original Meshy files remain unchanged in source storage.

## Scroll bridge

The dense donor preserves the ivory/gold rollers and useful ornament.
UV/hard-normal chart components did not form mechanical parts: moving those
components tore geometric seams. A deliberate cut-and-cap derivative retains
unaffected dense faces and closes the deck and roller volumes. Matched motion
proof at full, intermediate and minimum extension is available.

A separate material candidate preserves the mixed opaque ivory/gold PBR on the
rollers and uses physical glass on the deck. Hand-authored gold stars and frosted
rings remain separate material geometry inside the deck motion role. The render
adapter now binds materials by exact mesh name, independently of motion roles,
so these details will not become glass during integration.

Root review of the first transmissive candidate established actual transmission
with a blue/coral striped witness below the surface. It also found irregular
bright marks near the medallion and sharp stripe distortion below it. Removing
the provider normal map did not remove the defect; retained surface relief and
corner normals require further inspection. This candidate is not accepted for
runtime. A clean authored optical inset within the preserved dense frame is an
appropriate derivative if it removes this defect. Such a rebuild must be recorded
explicitly and compared against the source silhouette, thickness, detail and
matched reflection/transmission views.

## Rose and Amethyst crackle platforms

Both source donors are fused closed surfaces. Their raised gold lattice spans
the top and sides as well as corner fittings; treating all but the corners as
glass would discard important art. Gold isolation therefore needs reviewed
geometry regions, not thresholding metallic texture pixels.

The Rose contact screen found a largely flat surface, with small ornament
protrusions requiring a deliberate support decision. Amethyst contains deeper
shallow depressions; a flat collision plane alone does not certify a matching
visible landing. A Rose candidate is being prepared first to establish the
crystal-body, retained-lattice and closed-shard method before applying it to
Amethyst. Neither is a finished fracture asset yet.

## Remaining source masters

Packed projects preserve source meshes and high-resolution maps under a uniform
review transform. Platform reports distinguish provisional landing regions from
the resting base of gallery props. Finished contact bounds, glass materials,
fractures, animation roles and mobile runtime budgets remain separate acceptance
steps. Preview renders must never be presented as proof that those steps passed.
