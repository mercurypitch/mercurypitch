## Render review

- **Crescent:** selected. The frost approach bends gradually, and the recovery
  view places the planted pearl island on the inside of the arc while the dock
  and moving raft remain unobstructed. The following landing stays readable;
  the finale remains dissolved by the existing 9–14m fog.
- **Ribbon:** viable, but the first recovery asks for a more pronounced camera
  reversal. Its planted island and following dock are readable on opposite
  sides of the frame, which makes the S shape clear but busier.
- **Terrace:** mechanically forgiving and very legible, but its alternating
  offsets read more like a traversal exercise than a single scenic gesture.

Crystal planters stay at platform edges. The planted landmark sits on its own
small marble plinth outside the required movement line. Floor inlays identify
arrival, safe voice rests and the finale without adding collision or camera
occluders.
