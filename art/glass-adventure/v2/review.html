Terraces and arcades

Dimensional floor, stepped cornice and masonry arches. This studio proof shows the structural kit before the game's shared PBR maps; ornate capitals still require the selected detail pass.

Thin walls, separate gold

Blender-authored laurel goblet and rose vase, with real hollow shells and matching fragments. These are source models, not Meshy outputs. The other four new silhouettes are prepared as references.

Glass that breaks along its shape

Twenty-three closed irregular fragments for each new vessel, with gold attached to matching pieces. This is an expanded geometry proof; the game controls the break timing and movement.

Planted museum edges

Reusable foliage, flower and ivy clusters, with solid marble troughs and irregular stone roots. Shared vertex colors keep the repeated garden clusters inexpensive to draw.

The material palette

Sources and sampling notes
Surface Where it belongs What gives it depth
Ivory marble Walkable floor, columns, plinths Sparse veins, restrained normal detail and broad polished highlights.
Dark verde marble Floor inlays and recessed panels Deep green color, fine veining and a smooth finish.
Pale structural stone Arcades, undersides and island roots Rougher surface and deeper geometric shadows.
Champagne gold Rims, leaves, collars and canopy ribs Metallic reflections, with separate roughness for broad bands and fine trim.
Clear crystal Vases, goblets and display faces Visible wall thickness, neutral transmission and reflections of the museum.
