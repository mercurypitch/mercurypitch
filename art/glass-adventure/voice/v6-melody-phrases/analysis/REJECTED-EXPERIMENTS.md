# Rejected melody-bank experiments

These receipts are retained to explain discarded approaches. They do not
describe public assets or accepted playback.

## Detector-carrier experiment

`bank-pilot-pass1.json`, `bank-pilot-pass2.json`,
`runtime-verification-r48-p.json`, `runtime-verification-r50-p.json` and
`runtime-verification-r60-p.json` belong to the early low-carrier experiment.
The carrier made pitch detection easier by filling natural consonant gaps, so
that approach was rejected. Every public MP3 was later overwritten by the
carrier-free production or removed by `finalize_bank.py`.

## Carrier-free tuning pilots

Files containing `natural-span-remap`, `natural-vowel-remap`,
`natural-centered-nucleus`, `natural-110ms`, `sunlit-gentle` or
`sunlit-preserved-consonants` record bounded carrier-free experiments. They led
to the final natural consonant mapping but are not the release receipts.

The unqualified `runtime-verification.json`,
`runtime-verification-r50-p100.json`,
`runtime-verification-sunlit-steps-r50-p100.json` and the zero-row
`runtime-verification-gallery-arch-r50-p100.json` are superseded scratch
reports. The sample-rate-qualified root-50 reports are pilot evidence only.

The accepted evidence is limited to `bank-approved.json`,
`bank-intelligibility.json` and the three
`runtime-verification-full-sr*.json` reports. Owner listening remains pending.
