## Open after round 6: the amber glass (the owner's call)

The critic loop stopped by its rule of six. The rebuild beats the shipped file on outline
(silhouette IoU 0.990 against 0.930), lobes, shoulder band, clean shards and file size, and
loses to it on the glass's colour and on the stone. In the game the frosted amber reads as
satin butterscotch, and clear amber shows the gallery behind it; neither is the concept's lit
glass. The next attempt, if wanted, is the critic's: roughness 0.12 over an opaque liner inside
the cavity, graded from deep amber in the creases to light amber at the lobes' centres, so the
glass refracts a lit interior instead of the room; with it, the stone at about 55 x 70 mm and
the cup's cusps rising to 0.27 m to fill each crease. The shipped file stays until then.
