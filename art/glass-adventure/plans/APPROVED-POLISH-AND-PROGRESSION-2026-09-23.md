## Decisions selected for this batch

- Fit new shallow Frost/Glide sources to existing landing bounds while preserving
  decorative proportions and visible relief. Collision is a separate proxy.
- Coins remain finite exploration discoveries; no shop, grind or paid economy.
- First Light stays an ungraded prologue. Existing full learning galleries gain
  authored first/two/three-star profiles. First clear is easy; either harder tier
  can be attempted afterward. Difficulty does not force a wider vocal range.
- Keep legacy accuracy results as portrait performance, never relabel them as
  harder difficulty clears. Preserve historical Cloudway eligibility.
- A level's tier requires every required exhibit and its exit in one compatible
  attempt. Optional discoveries and portraits survive replay/profile changes.
- Longer holds belong at selected mastery exhibits, not every vase. Melody,
  timing and tolerance are authored configuration, not buried constants.
- Collection covers existing galleries; no empty ten-level album or fabricated
  future achievements. New worlds and map expansion wait at item 9.
- Player recording is explicit opt-in, local by default, replayable and deletable.
  A take can be downloaded from its preview for deliberate sharing; no automatic uploads.
- The ending encore is optional and cosmetic. It never blocks a learned lesson,
  takes away a portrait or penalizes choosing to continue.
