## What the hosts do

- Talk on their own lines (mouth by the line's loudness, sound arcs at half strength).
- Sing their own sounds: Merc `gauge`, `listen` (A3) and `twinkle`; Tavi `demo` (on the page's
  demo line, to the cent) and `listen` (A4); full sound arcs.
- Listen to the other's lines: the renderer's `listen` (one eye closed, leaning toward the
  speaker, no arcs) for the first 3 s of each line (the whole line if shorter), then `idle`, eyes
  open and held on the speaker, until the line ends (DECISIONS 161).
- Idle, turned to the drawing, through every sound neither makes (easy, pushed, push, the starts,
  starts, the other's sounds), the pauses, and the viewer's breathe, ticks and hold: no arcs.
- Talk in a cycle: the mouth opens and shuts with each syllable (5.5 times a second from each
  word's start), never as wide as a sung note; the arcs pulse with it (DECISIONS 201).
- Breathe in before a phrase: the mouth opens 0.15 s before the first word after every pause of
  0.25 s or more. Blink in the pauses: the talker as the voice stops, the listener after, wherever
  the renderer's blinks reach (DECISIONS 189; the list is `merc/final-report.md`).
- Keep their eyes at rest across every cut: nothing moves an eye from 0.2 s before a scene
  boundary to 0.2 s after it; a `listen` that would start on a cut starts 0.2 s after it (DECISIONS 203).

## Where the feet sit in a pass's frame

Measured on every dumped frame with the root at rest (lab/merc/dump/), as a fraction of the
frame's height from its top (the lowest row with alpha 128 or more: the nearer foot's sole) and
of its width (the middle of the opaque body):

| Drawn square | Frames | Sole, lowest row (min to max) | Body top (alpha 128) | Body centre, x |
| ------------ | ------ | ----------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------- |
| 225 px | 3 | 0.836 (0.836 to 0.836) | 0.249 | 0.500 |

Both hosts render facing screen right with the same camera (`front`), so both have the same
feet: **the feet line is 0.836 of the frame's height** (the median nearer sole above; it moves
by about one drawn pixel with the pose), the farther foot's sole about 0.02 higher (perspective:
the body is turned 0.32 rad), and the body is centred at **0.500** of the width. The floor under
the body's centre (the root, y 0) projects to 0.789; the soles sit below it because the feet
stand in front of the root.

## Placing a pass in a scene

- **The tag.** A static `<video id="<scene>-<pass>" data-merc="<pass>" class="clip" muted playsinline>`
  in the scene, `data-merc` the pass id (`m-blue`, never a chunk id); `python3 tools/merc-tags.py
  <scene>` writes its `src` (the scene's own chunk), `data-start`, `data-duration` and
  `data-media-start`. A reflection tag beside it carries `data-merc-refl` (trap 4).
- **The box.** Show the video in a square box of side S (CSS px), the frame filling it (the video
  is square). S is the shown size: 300 in the corners, 240 small (S6 to S9), 400 on the end card,
  420 in S1. A chunk is rendered at the largest S its scene uses, so a scene only ever scales a
  pass down (S6 to S9 show 300 renders at 240), never up.
- **Standing on y 840.** With the feet point at (x, 840): `left = x - 0.5 S`, `top = 840 - 0.836 S`
  (300: top 589.2; 240: top 639.4; 400: top 505.6; 420: top 488.9). Scale the box about the
  feet point, `transform-origin: 50% 83.6%`, so a size change in place keeps the feet on the
  floor. The body box HANDOFFS gives (0.53 S wide, 0.64 S tall above the feet) is the opaque body
  and its glow.
- **Mirroring Tavi.** Every one of Tavi's passes (m01-orange, m-orange) is shown with `scaleX(-1)`
  about the box's vertical centre line (on the video, or with the origin above); the feet point
  stays at 0.5 S, so nothing else changes. Merc's passes are never mirrored (his hops go right).
- **The reflection.** The same frame flipped about the feet line, y 840 on screen (`scaleY(-1)`
  about 83.6 % of the box), at 16 % (HANDOFFS § The floor).
- **Root motion.** The renderer moves Merc's root during a hop: x from 0 to +0.38 world units
  through the air (49.6 px at S 300: 0.4353 x S px a unit; the feet measured 35 to 39 drawn px
  on a landed hop against 37.2), held until the next hop snaps it back to 0, then after the second
  hop eased back to 0 over about half a second. A scene that carries him itself cancels it: on each
  frame, shift the box by `-root[i][0] x 0.4353 x S` px (the opposite sign on a mirrored pass), frame
  i counted from the chunk's first frame. Then the box's path is the scene's own tween, and he lands
  exactly on (700, 840) and (1290, 840). The root's y is the jump itself (leaps, hops, the end
  card): leave it. Only the hops move a root's x: m-blue-s13 is the one chunk whose x moves; every
  other chunk's x is 0 on every frame (only `hop` sets the root's x in the renderer; the leaps
  and the end-card hop are jumps in place, y only), so an all-zero table is right, not missing.
- **The root table, for the hosts layer.** `assets/merc/roots.js` (written by `tools/merc-roots.py`
  from the sidecars) sets `window.MERC_ROOTS`, one entry a chunk keyed by its id (the file's name;
  the chunk `tools/merc-tags.py` gives scene `sNN-...` is `<pass>-sNN`; m01-blue and m01-orange
  are keyed by the pass): `{ frames: [[x, y], ...], t0, fps }`, `t0` the film time of `frames[0]`.
  It loads after `assets/scenes.js` and before the scenes (the lead adds it to `scripts.txt`). A
  scene passes its own chunk's entry to the hosts layer:

  ```js
  L.mercGate(layer, { sid: 's13-recap', pass: 'm-blue', tag: 's13-recap-m-blue', x: 110, size: 300,
    moves: [/* the two hops' carries, power1.in */], root: window.MERC_ROOTS['m-blue-s13'] })
  ```

  Passing it in every scene is harmless (all zeros cancel nothing); only S13's Merc needs it.
- **Leaps.** Each long pass starts on its leap (`celebrate`, frame 0). Until the scene carries the
  box in, it is off frame: Tavi from beyond the right edge, Merc from beyond the left; both land
  in their corners 0.80 s before their first word (HANDOFFS: `leapDx` 300 px, the carry in the air).
- **Size changes and moves** are the scene's, in screen space (the passes never change size).
- **Timing.** A 60 fps pass in the 60 fps master: film frame n shows pass frame n - P, P the
  pass's first frame (round(filmStart x 60)). The tags sit half a pass frame from every rounding
  edge, so no film frame falls on a pass-frame boundary: `python3 tools/merc-tags.py --margins`
  gives the nearest boundary for every tag (6.8 ms or more; `--check` fails under 4 ms).
- **The sidecar** (`assets/merc/<file>-merc.json`): `from`, `to`, `frames`, `fps`, `filmStart`,
  `size`, `drawn`, the render's `msPerFrame`, `renderSeconds` and `prerollSeconds`, `crescentFixed`
  (frames the crescent guard re-rendered, trap 10), the `pin` (Chromium, Playwright, the bundle's
  sha256 and commit) and `root`, one [x, y] a frame.
