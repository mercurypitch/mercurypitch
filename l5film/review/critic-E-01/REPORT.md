     answer to "stays there" is on screen complete for about 0.2 s.
   - From 27.87 to 29.60 s (1.73 s) the stage is empty apart from Merc hopping, and the audio is
     digital silence (-91 dB).
   - From 29.60 to 30.87 s the panel shows only "Practise it."; "Voice Mirror" lands at 30.87 to
     31.13 s. The board has it 0.4 s after the panel.
   - Fix: start the exit no earlier than about 28.1 s, so the finished line holds at least 0.5 s.
     Land "Voice Mirror" 0.4 s after the panel instead of on the spoken word.
2. **S13, 50.5 to 64.8 s: the "Ten minutes, gently…" line is wrong.**

   - It overhangs the card's right edge (1110) by 34 px, and its cap tops are 8 px under the card's
     foot (790).

   - Fix: break it after "most days." onto baselines 822 and 862, as HANDOFFS sets them.
3. **S13, 1.7 to 27.7 s: the recap is sparse.**
   - The figure covers about 2.9 % of the frame and the traces about 6.3 %.
   - The dimmed band sits there as a dead strip for 17 s, the lower third is empty, and the same
     layout repeats three times.

5. **S13, 8.4 to 19.4 s: the traces carry no ILLUSTRATION chip.** The board's Codes and its Honesty

7. **S14, 6.0 to 9.7 s: the tease line sits 9 px under "NEXT TIME".** The two left edges also differ
