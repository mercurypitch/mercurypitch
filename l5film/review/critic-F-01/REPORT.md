cuts, a clear end card. But for long stretches it is a thin strip or a block of small type floating
in a dark frame, and it fails the frozen-time bar. It would look weaker than Lessons 1 and 2.

the 0.2 s sheets; 15 fps sheets at 17 transitions; frame-exact sheets at 5 events; crops in

   - 349-406 and 422-462: small type on the left, a bare ring about 280 px across (until 406), and

   - 42-66: the promise and the recall leave the lower half empty, with swatches about 110 px.
   - 233.0-243.9: the trace box sits empty under "onset" and "Air first".

     the frame. Put the ring at r 260 or more in the right half from 353. Remove the band in
