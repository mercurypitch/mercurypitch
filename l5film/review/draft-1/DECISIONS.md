Numbered, stable ids, grouped by topic. A decision that changes keeps its number and says what
replaced it. The writer's record for draft 1 (18 November 2026): critics never read it. Facts are
cited by `FACTS.md` number; lines by `script.json` id (b = Merc, o = Tavi).

## Structure

   CURIOSITY-MOMENTS § 3.2), Tavi's guess follows at about 0:05, Merc asks the page's predict aloud
   with its two choices by about 0:16 and holds 2.0 s, then the pushed take and the reveal. On the
   page Merc's "Here's one note, sung easily." comes before the take; in the film the take comes
   first, so the frame and the sound open the film (FACTS 82).
2. **The hook follows the reveal, as Tavi's question** ("But singers are told to "support" a long
   note. If that doesn't mean pushing, what keeps it steady?", done by about 0:40). Said before the
   predict it would hint at the answer (CURIOSITY-MOMENTS § 1 rule 4); after it, it is the next
   question, and it picks up Lesson 4's last two lines. Later than § 3.2's 0:15 for "the question",
   but the first question lands at 0:05 and the predict's by 0:16, inside rule 1's 30 s; Lesson 4's
   hook ended at 0:34 by estimate and 0:38 voiced, and passed six film rounds.
3. **The recall follows the promise and asks one question**, Lesson 3's question 4 ("How loud
   should you sing when you practise matching?"), Tavi asking and Merc answering with the page's
   feedback. It is the one this lesson builds on: a quiet note is the right note, and loud and high
   are different, which chapter 1 complicates. Question 3 (the A2) stays on the page, as Lesson 4's
   film carried one of its page's two (Lesson 4 film DECISIONS 3). The four words are on screen
   only, each defined in its chapter (FACTS 83).
4. **The page's five steps are five chapters**, each with a chapter card ("01 Why pushing moves the

## Teaching

7. **The misconception is refuted three ways, each by something heard or seen**: the pushed take
   climbing out of the band (cold open), the breath figure and the gauge (chapter 2: the diaphragm
   breathes in; full lungs already push), and Tavi's own steady line, which is not straight
   (chapter 5). Merc never calls a guess wrong; "Not the pushing." and "That's a common mix-up." are
   the strongest words.
8. **No host sings a pushed note.** The takes and the Push note are the page's drawings of a rule
   (FACTS 72, 73), voiced by nobody, while both hosts watch; the coach lists them "for listening
   only", and a host pushing would model it.
9. **Tavi sings the hold demo, an octave up** (`demo`, FACTS 80, 86): rule 16's order (a model, Tavi
   tries, then the viewer) and rule 15's (she is surprised by her own scoop, and it is fine). The
   contour is the page's, unchanged; only the octave and the singer are the film's. If the lead
   prefers the page's form, the same contour on A3 with no singer is one line of the mixer.
10. **The hook is answered in one place, in the page's words**: b20 (the mix-up card's better idea)
    and Tavi's o13, which joins it to chapters 3 to 5. Both say a steady note *needs* steady air,
    never that steady air keeps it steady (FACTS 10; § 5).
11. **Step 1's Notice, "Keep Push still, and so does the line.", is not voiced**: over a film it
    would read as "steady air holds a note still" (FACTS 84). The picture shows the toy's line
    returning with the slider.
12. **Step 3's breathe-along stays on the page.** The film's figure breathes once, slowly, under
    the cues, with no ring, no count and no invitation, so the chapter needs no care lines of its
    own; the care line moves to the turn (b35), and the turn's "Breathe in, low and quiet" is where
    the viewer breathes. Cut for length (6); it is the first thing to restore if time allows.
13. **The catch is for listening only**: b24 ends "Just listen to these.", the catch is tagged
    "listen only", and nothing asks for one (the coach's Step 4 item).
14. **No number on Tavi's hold.** The readouts would come from a replica of the page's reader, not
    the reader (FACTS 80); s10 shows a "settled" marker without a number.
15. **The recap asks the page's four questions, open**, each answered by its correct answer and,
    where it helps, a few words of its feedback (FACTS 88); Tavi asks question 2, as she asked one
    in Lesson 4. They keep the page's order (chapters 2, 1, 4 and 5), so the first answer is not
    the last thing the viewer heard.

## Safety and the turn

16. **One ten-second hold.** The page offers fifteen only after "Easy" or "Workable", with Again and
    a cap of three tries (FACTS 52); a film cannot ask first, so it offers neither, and the promise
    stops at ten (FACTS 82, 87). The move of up to four semitones and "Done for today" stay on the
    page too.
17. **The care comes first, in the page's order**: the task (b34), Lesson 3's care line (b35), the
    comfort line (b36), the comfort check (b37), then the voice choice, the briefing, the stop-early
    line and the stop line, all before a note is sung.
18. **The comfort check is voiced word for word** (b37, 17 s), not shortened and not left on screen
    alone: it is the gate before the film's only singing, and its wording is Lesson 3's, which the
    coach signs. On screen it is short labels under the bold comfort line.
19. **The doctor lines are on screen, not voiced**, as Lesson 4's were: word for word with their
    parentheses, in small type from b35 to the end of the turn, full size from "Uncomfortable?"
    through a 2.5 s `rest`, all above the caption band; also in the YouTube description. On screen
    they stand exactly as the lesson words them; voiced, the parentheses would change.
20. **The model note is sung in both octaves at once** (`listen`: Merc's A3 and Tavi's A4, 2.0 s), so
    a viewer who chose either voice hears theirs straight before the breath; the page plays the
    chosen one. `pick` plays each alone first, a Lower tap and a Higher tap, so the choice is heard.
21. **Room tone only under the hold**, no pad: the page's hold has nothing playing while it is open,
    and Lesson 4's turn kept only room tone. Lesson 1 put its pad (on A) under its hum; here a pad
    would be a drone the page's task does not give.
22. **No sound marks the ring's start or close.** Merc says "Breathe in." and the briefing said "sing
    straight away"; the ring turning and closing is the picture's cue. A soft synthesised tick at
    each end would help a viewer who sings with their eyes closed, but nothing in the page or the
    facts has one (open question).
23. **The warm-up stays on the page** (Lesson 3's three sighs and the optional lip trill): the
    CURRICULUM allows it "at the vocal coach's discretion", and the film's one turn is ten seconds at
    speaking volume. If the coach wants it before the hold, Lesson 3's words and Merc's sigh (E4 to
    A3) and Tavi's (E5 to A4) fit in about 20 s (FACTS 53, 77).
24. **"How did that feel?" keeps the page's labels today**, Easy, Workable, Effortful, Uncomfortable;
    the coach decides the proposed Easy, OK, Hard work, Uncomfortable, which would change b43 to b45
    only. Each answer's line is the page's.
25. **Nothing listens.** No microphone, no line for the viewer, no reading, and the hosts still
    during the hold; the chrome playhead is a clock (FACTS 87; BRIEF, Must never appear).

## Sound

    makes (FACTS 79). No Echo samples: the takes, the Push note and Tavi's hold are contours, and
    using the samples for the plain A's alone would put two timbres in one film.
27. **Tavi's hold follows the drawn line to the cent**: `demoCents`, the voice's own vibrato off, so
    the wave heard is the wave drawn (FACTS 80). The page lets the voice's vibrato carry the wave
    from the note's start; the film makes sound and line one.
28. **The bed is an open fifth on A**, in the transitions and the dressed pauses, never under a line,
    a sound, the listening beat, the breath, the hold or the rest (FACTS 81). In A, the title and
    end chimes sit with it, as in Lesson 1.
29. **The tease is silent.** Playing "Twinkle" from C would set up Lesson 6, but playing the moved
    copy would answer its predict; the beads carry the set-up.
