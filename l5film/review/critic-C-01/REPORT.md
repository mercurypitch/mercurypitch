## Problems, ranked

1. **S9 4.6–15.6, 17.9–24.7 and 27.0–31.4 (about 22 s): empty frame.** The right two-thirds of the frame is a hairline in a dark field (`crops/s09-9.50.png`), and a 340 px folds box is the only subject. Even 20.3–24.6 measures as low motion, with only a host moving. Fix:
   - Draw the strip as a lit lane, like the stack rows' boxes (chrome at about 6 %, x 700–1600, y 320–540).
   - Keep each finished trace on screen at 30 % until the next one draws over it.
   - Scale the folds box up by about 1.4.
2. **S10 58.2–60.9: the 10h demonstration does not read.** The slider and line run for only about 1.85 s. They draw a 190 px scribble in which the push bumps cannot be told from the vibrato (`crops/s10-60.20.png`). "a change in the air moves it" is at full strength for only 0.6 s (60.07–60.67, measured per frame) and is gone by 60.93. Fix:
   - Start the slider on "needs" (56.08).
   - Use one push, 50 to 75 to 50, with each leg 0.8 s or longer.
   - Land "a change in the air moves it" on "because" (58.17) and start the fade no earlier than 60.85.
3. **Sound: every example in the clips is digital silence.** That covers start-air (15.63–17.23), the catch (24.66–26.26), together (31.39–32.99) and the three back to back (37.96–43.16) in S9, and Tavi's 10 s demo (9.47–19.47) in S10. There is also no whoosh or pad under any chapter card: 0–2.3 s is silent in all three clips. In total that is 15.3 s of silence in S9 and 14.4 s in S10, so the picture's sync with the sound cannot be verified. Fix: re-render the lab clips with the synthesised examples and the act sounds.
4. **S9 48.8–57.1: "neither" and "the easy start" share a baseline (y about 565).** They read as "neither the easy start", which says the opposite of the point. Fix: set "the easy start" under the "Together" row name (right-aligned to x 660, baseline about 700).
5. **S9 53.2–55.9: the "h" cannot be read.** It is 40 % ink-2 behind row 3's bright wave strokes (`crops/s09-54.00.png`). Fix: dim the first 200 px of row 3 to 25 % while the "h" shows, and raise the "h" to about 70 %.
6. **Chapter openings go near-empty.** In S8 (2.23–3.2) the only subject is a dim, small figure in transit. In S9 (2.23–3.4) it is a 20–40 px ring. Fix:
   - Start the carried object's move 0.3 s before the card leaves.
   - Bring the figure to full strength at the start of its move.
   - Grow the folds in 0.5 s.
7. **S9 33.47–34.27: the stack's row names land while trace 3 is still travelling.** At 33.95 the together trace, with its gold tick, sits beside "A catch" (`crops/s09-33.95.png`). At 33.6 "Air first" touches the corner of the still-large folds box. Fix: land the three names after the trace has settled (from about 34.4, 0.4 s apart), and shrink the folds first.
8. **S10 51.33–51.73 ("No."): everything fades within 0.2 s of everything else.** The guess, "steady: settles into the band and stays", Tavi's line and every label go together, so on mute "No" reads as negating the whole chart. Fix: strike through the guess on "No.", hold 0.4 s, then clear the rest.
9. **S9 38.0–57.1: "LISTEN ONLY" belongs to no row.** It sits 10 px under row 1 and 10 px over row 2, right-aligned under "ILLUSTRATION". It reads as row 1's tag or the stack's, not the catch's, so LAB-CHECKS 13 fails on reading. Fix: place it under "A catch" (right-aligned to x 660, centred y about 495).
10. **S10 33.6–39.1: the vibrato bracket cannot be seen.** It is a 2 px chrome mark lying on the 4 px violet wave (`crops/s10-35.50.png`). Separately, the "stays" bracket (42.1 onwards) sits 7 px above the mint edge and reads as a doubled edge. Fix:
    - Replace the vibrato bracket with ink hairlines at crest and trough height across about 240 px, with end ticks.
    - Move the "stays" bracket 16 px or more above the edge.
11. **Holds exceed the per-30-s total.**
