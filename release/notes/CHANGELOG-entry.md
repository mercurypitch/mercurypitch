## [0.9.22] - 2026-10-DD

Find Your Pitch opens with its first two free lessons, each with its film,
at mercurypitch.com/learn. New visitors answer four quick questions and get
a plan, which Home keeps for a week.

### Added

- **Find Your Pitch: free lessons on notes and singing.** The first two are
  open: _What is a note?_ and _Twelve notes_, 8 and 13 minutes. Home's
  Lessons link opens the first, and mercurypitch.com/learn maps the season.
- **Each lesson is a page you play.** Guess before you listen, play with the
  sound toys, sing along with the hosts, Merc and Tavi, then try a
  four-question quick check. No microphone needed.
- **Watch each lesson's episode on its page.** They run 5:35 and 7:19, with
  captions that name who is speaking and chapters you tap to jump to.
- **A short tour on your first lesson page.** Five steps show you around.
  The question mark at the top right, or Tour this page in the lesson menu,
  plays it again.
- **A lesson menu on every lesson page.** The Lesson button at the top lists
  every lesson in the season and every step of this one, and shows where
  you are.
- **Your place is kept.** The season map and the card that ends each lesson
  offer Start lesson, Continue: step 3 or Read again, saved in this browser.
- **A page for karaoke questions.** mercurypitch.com/karaoke-app answers the
  first ones: is it free, your own songs, singing with friends, and TVs.
- **Four questions, then a plan.** What you play, what you're working
  toward, what gets in the way and what you've tried; skip any of them. The
  answers pick up to three stops: Start here, Learn and Play.
- **Your plan stays close.** Home shows it for a week, ticks each stop you
  finish and starts its tour on it. Guide opens it again, and a lesson page
  offers Back to your plan.
- **Lesson questions are counted anonymously.** The first answer to each
  quick-check and recall question in a visit is sent as right or wrong,
  with no account or device id. Nothing is sent if you declined, nor in the
  EEA, the UK or Switzerland until you say yes.

### Changed

- **The Guided Warmup ends without a grade.** You see "Warmup complete", or
  "Warmup ended" if you stop early, and the steps you finished. A full
  warm-up still counts toward your streak.
- **The vocal range test asks for your highest comfortable note.** Your voice
  twin is a broad guide now, not a verdict.
- **Coming back to the range test shows then and now.** "Since 7 Jul: span 14
  to 16 semitones" replaces the arrows, or "Similar today" when nothing
  moved.
- **Bass-baritone, by name.** A range that sits as much in Bass as in
  Baritone, like E2 to G4, now reads Bass-baritone. Your voice twin stays
  the same.
- **The hold drills stop asking for your longest hold.** Long Note and Pitch
  Hold let you choose how long to hold.
- **The Siren scores a smooth glide.** A slow, even slide now earns its
  glide part. Your Best, grade and play count start again from your next
  run, and the high-notes and range challenges start new attempts.
- **One welcome at a time.** What's New, a tour offer, the cookie bar and
  your plan take turns instead of arriving together.
- **Skipping the welcome asks first.** It names what you'd miss, such as
  your range, your voiceprint and your plan, with Keep going and Yes, skip.
- **Learn is back in the sidebar,** beside Guide and Tour, and Home's
  header has Lessons in its place. On a touch screen, Home's header buttons
  and these three are 44 px tall.
- **The feedback form says "No account needed."** It no longer calls itself
  anonymous: when you're signed in, what you send is kept with your account.
- **A wrong address gets a proper page.** One note has drifted off its stave
  over a night sky, with the way home and a link to every room.
- **A lesson's practice link opens its room.** On a first visit, the room the
  lesson sent you to opens straight away, without the intro.

### Fixed

- **The intro names a note you held.** When your note wavered between two,
  it could name the one in between. Now it names one you sang.
- **Your score shows over the Zen stage.** After a run with the mic, the
  score card opened hidden behind the stage and took the keyboard.
- **Space and M stay in the survey.** Typing in the feedback form or a
  confirm no longer pauses or mutes the song behind it, and Escape keeps
  what you typed.
- **On a slower device, overlays keep the app behind them.** The mixer's
  tour, Zen's song list and the Learn dialog no longer open over an empty
  screen, and the mixer's tour shows the song list it points at.
- **Your own songs get the pitch guide on a phone.** A song you separated
  now finds its notes as it streams, and Find my key works on it.
- **On a phone, the keyboard leaves your typing in view** in the plan's
  questions, and the email reminder's close button sits in its corner.
- **A link to the Map opens the Map.** Followed as the app was starting, it
  could land you on Home.
- **Merc no longer holds up the page while he gets ready.** If he isn't
  ready after 10 seconds, his picture stays.
- **Keyboards and screen readers.** The Zen and phone karaoke stages keep
  the focus inside, and Enter moves a tour on instead of restarting it.
- **Smaller things.** Interval Trainer's card no longer says it sings
  intervals below the note, Long Note and Pitch Hold get tags that match,
  screen readers hear Piano Night's name as its heading, the Ear Lab's tap
  pad counts a screen reader's tap, and a lyrics pick that brings nothing
  keeps the list and says why.
